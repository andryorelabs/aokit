# Brand

The brand rules every AndryOre repo follows. The public text lives in andryore-web; aokit's own kit is in [docs/brand](../brand/README.md). Index: [standards](README.md). Terms follow [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md).

Each rule is marked **must** or **should**, gives one sentence, then the reason.

- **G1** (must) `docs/brand` holds the kit file set, `brand:export` is tested, and a contrast test covers the palette. Reason: a brand that is not tested decays into guesses.
- **G2** (must) Each product keeps its own palette and type, and the names are "AndryOre" (brand), "Andry Orellana" (person) and `andryorelabs` (org). Reason: products stay distinct while the names stay unambiguous.
- **G3** (must) The credit appears in the README, the package author and the site footer, and sites live at `<name>.andryore.dev`. Reason: every product points back to its author in the same places.

Five marks on one 32-unit grid, in the Soot palette (ink plus a muted tone, no accent colour) and the Geist wordmark. Every mark has to survive three tests: a 16px favicon, six rows of block characters in the terminal, and one colour. The grid idea nods to AndryOre without copying andryore-web's mark.

Reply with the mark you want (M1 to M5) and any tweak: a different offset, rounder or squarer corners, a heavier stroke.
